# Hey, I'm Sebastian.

Integrated Engineering student at UBC, mechanical by training, and I write the software too. Last summer I built an AI requirements tool for the parking and driver-assistance team at Porsche in Weissach. At UBC I lead Steering and Controls on Formula Electric and run the IGEN 3D print shop.

Most of my projects started as personal problems I got frustrated enough to fix. At some point I realised that if I had the problem, someone else probably did too. So I built it properly, for both of us.

**Looking for an 8-month co-op starting May 2027**, somewhere hardware and software genuinely overlap.

### Shipped

**[BaseMark](https://github.com/seb-krols/PrintMark)** - Nobody can tell PLA from PETG once a part leaves the printer. A PrusaSlicer add-on that stamps the material and a part number into the underside of every print. Running in the UBC IGEN print shop.

**[GraphConvert](https://github.com/seb-krols/graph-convert)** - AI tools can't read graphs, they just see images. Right-click any graph, get precise (x, y) data points in JSON, CSV, or TXT. Live on the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cepidnjndbmllpkigekbocfjakmfjlko).

**[Spotlore](https://github.com/seb-krols/spotlore-showcase)** - Started as a hand-coded Valentine's Day gift with 51 locations delivered with flowers. Rebuilt it into a full product: cinematic map tours, six themes, password-protected sharing. Live at [spotlore.app](https://spotlore.app).

**[Email Triage](https://github.com/seb-krols/email_triage)** - My inbox was half anxiety, half productivity. One email at a time, one decision at a time, with AI suggesting the action and me approving every one. Local-first, with a two-minute demo mode.

**[Oh Shit It's Due](https://github.com/seb-krols/oh-shit-its-due)** - Canvas notifications are too polite. Three escalating panic levels, push that works when the app is closed, Canvas tokens secured server-side.

**[BackBreeze](https://github.com/seb-krols/back-breeze)** - Singapore is humid and my back was constantly soaked. A parametric generator for 3D-printable TPU backpack inserts, with a live 3D web configurator.

### Building now

**Tremor-suppression wearable** - My capstone. A wrist device that detects hand tremor in real time (6 to 12 Hz, from IMUs on an ESP32) and counters it with electrical stimulation, on a PCB I designed for it.

**VIGIL** - "Industry 4.0 for humans." Every signal about my own health in one store (WHOOP, glucose, location, audio), with an insight engine that only reports correlations that survive real statistics, and stays quiet when nothing does.
